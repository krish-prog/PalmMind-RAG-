Company Overview

Northwind Labs (a fictional company used for this demo) builds conversational chatbots and document intelligence products for businesses.

Office Hours

The office is open from 9 AM to 6 PM, Sunday to Friday. Saturday is a holiday.

Hiring Process

The hiring process has three stages: a take-home technical task, a technical interview, and a final HR discussion. Technical interviews last about 45 minutes and focus on the candidate's submitted task.

Benefits

Employees receive health insurance, paid leave, and a yearly learning budget for courses and conferences.
